What the game is about

Minimalist, readable, and deeply sequence-driven

Shadow Arrow Flee is built around a simple but satisfying question: which arrow can escape right now?

Every tap changes the board. One smart move can free a whole chain. One rushed move can close the last open lane and force a full rethink. The result is a puzzle rhythm that feels calm on the surface and sharp underneath.

The game is designed for players who enjoy logic games, visual planning, maze reading, and short but meaningful puzzle sessions.

1. Scan the board

Look for the arrow whose head already has a clear path out of the maze.

2. Tap in the right order

Every removal changes what can move next, so sequencing matters as much as recognition.

3. Solve without deadlocking

When the board locks up, restart quickly and try a cleaner route.

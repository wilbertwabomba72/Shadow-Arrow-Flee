Privacy Policy

Shadow Arrow Flee

This Privacy Policy explains how Shadow Arrow Flee handles information when you use the app.

Effective date: April 27, 2026

1. Personal information

Shadow Arrow Flee does not require account creation and does not ask you to submit personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or location.

2. Data collection

The app is designed to work offline and does not include in-app analytics, advertising SDKs, or user tracking features.

  • No advertising identifiers are collected by the app.
  • No third-party analytics events are sent by the app.
  • No login or account profile data is collected by the app.

3. Local device storage

To make gameplay convenient, the app stores a small amount of information locally on your device, including:

  • your highest unlocked level,
  • which levels you have completed, and
  • whether background music is enabled.

This information stays on the device and is used only to preserve your progress and settings.

4. Permissions and hardware access

The app uses standard audio playback for music and sound effects. It does not request access to contacts, photos, camera, microphone, location services, or motion data for gameplay.

5. Children’s privacy

Shadow Arrow Flee does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults. Because the app does not provide account creation or direct user submission features, there is no in-app channel for personal data collection.

6. Third-party services

The app itself does not embed third-party advertising or analytics services. If you download the app from the App Store, Apple may process information according to its own terms and privacy practices. Please review Apple’s policies separately for platform-level services.

7. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app’s features change in the future. If that happens, the updated policy will be published with a new effective date.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the contact information provided in the app’s App Store listing.
